# Growth teardown

Identify the product, the user's decision and the conversion that matters. A domain alone is enough for exploratory research. If the user names one surface, market or channel, investigate it directly. Match the deliverable to the request: a documented explanation may be complete without a plan.

**Trace a real route.** Through AgentRouter, inspect at least one first-party page, shared artifact or public example behind the proposed mechanism. Identify who encounters it, where, the visible next action, and where the evidence ends. When a feature, paid tier, locale or embedding mode changes what visitors see, check the relevant variant before generalizing. Distinguish an option documented by the product from a control actually visible on a customer’s page. Cite the source for each material step.

Choose additional evidence for the question. Search rankings with landing URLs can support discovery, channel estimates can compare traffic, and public ads can show a pitch and destination. Treat a branded query as existing demand. Give date and geography for measured data. A visible path supports a mechanism; its contribution to traffic, paid customers or revenue needs separate evidence. Label any inference. Stop when the open question is answered or the available evidence runs out.

**When the user wants an experiment**, adapt the mechanism to their audience, resources, existing distribution and time window. State which prerequisite they already have or would need. Give an actionable asset or change, who will see it first, and the action that counts as success. Define the denominator and tracking window, plus a decision rule appropriate to the sample. If attribution matters, explain how to associate the intended outcome with the experiment. Identify what a small sample can establish; a proposed threshold is a planning choice. If the user requests multiple options, compare them on the constraints they named.

**Tool use.** Check current contracts, prices and geographic coverage. Use authorized read-only lookups and equivalents that preserve the requested scope. Stop after pricing for a preview. Recover an uncertain run by ID or identical replay with the same key. Link the evidence; report research charges separately from any proposed marketing spend.
